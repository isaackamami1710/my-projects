About

Quantitative training, applied to a real insurance book

I am a health insurance underwriter with a quantitative background. My work involves assessing medical risk, applying underwriting terms, pricing renewals against claims experience, and keeping member records accurate across policy administration systems.

Alongside that, I study and build with data: statistical modelling, time-series forecasting, machine learning and financial engineering. My MSc work covered demand forecasting, portfolio analysis, volatility and regime modelling, and quantile-regression approaches to financial risk.

The direction I am moving in is the intersection of the two — insurance analytics and risk modelling supported by data science. Industry knowledge plus technical execution is a more useful combination than either one on its own, and it is where I do my best work.

I work mainly in Python and SQL, with Excel as the everyday tool for pricing models and reporting, and I am building out Power BI and machine learning depth.
